# Accepted-image recovery policy

Owner decision, 2026-11-03: "nećemo više provjeravati povrat s07 jer nema smisla".

For future returns to the same already accepted s07 image, do not request
repeated physical cold-power cycles, listening, Pause or transport-CUE checks,
or owner confirmation of those recovery tests. Close the write/recovery step
with the existing automatic written-span and whole-flash readback. Keep the
exact accepted app, complete executed ancestry, preserved sector tails and
fresh identity/security/predecessor checks. Do not weaken the programmer.

If starting the restored application needs a reset, use a separately reviewed
and registered software boot action where supported. Keep it outside the
unchanged no-reset writer and record whether it was issued. Do not treat an
unissued boot action as a running application. Do not ask the owner to perform
another recovery qualification sequence. The present s07 is already running
and owner-confirmed from the campaign completed before this decision.

Candidate diagnostic/functional measurements keep their own registered
requirements, including physical power removal when the measurement itself
is a cold boot. Register future campaign exits with automatic s07 readback
and the required software boot action, without the removed manual tests.

This decision supersedes older README/handoff/tool instructions requiring
repeated s07 cold/listening acceptance. Earlier byte-frozen runbooks, bindings,
raw owner replies and archives remain immutable historical evidence. They do
not reinstate the removed future manual recovery gates. A changed or newly
promoted image has its own qualification scope; this policy concerns returns
to the same accepted s07.
