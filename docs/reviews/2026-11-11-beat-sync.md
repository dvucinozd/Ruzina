# JC1060: beat sync and the Beat Loop and Beat Jump pad LEDs

Date: 2026-11-11. Branch: `ogranak/jc1060-beat-sync`, from `main` at `253f5ad`.

## Problem

SYNC matched only the tempo. The legacy engine called the deck state without the
tracks' analysis, so the deck state's phase alignment never ran. That alignment works
by seeking, and a seek on a playing deck would leave the deck behind by its own delay:
the reopen, then a cushion of at least 93 ms.

In Beat Loop and Beat Jump modes the pads gave no light at all
([controller LEDs](2026-11-06-controller-leds.md) left them out, as the native
firmware does).

## Change

- **Grid:** the load task already reads each loaded track's analysis for the screen.
  It now also hands the beat grid to the deck engine, through a second one-place
  mailbox. The USB task takes the grid outside the audio fill.
- **Phase:** after SYNC, once both decks play, the engine finds where the reference is
  in its bar: the beat number and how far into the beat. It picks the nearest place in
  the synced track with the same beat number at the same fraction of the beat. The
  reader jumps there within audio it already has:
  - backward through the history;
  - forward through the ring. Skipped frames are not kept, so the history starts again
    after the jump.

  The jump uses the loop crossfade. If the nearest place is not in the buffered audio,
  the reader takes the nearest one the other way.
- **When it waits:** a paused deck aligns when PLAY starts it. The jump waits during a
  loop and while the jog is touched. Without both grids, SYNC matches the tempo only.
- **Log:** the jump is load event 40, a jump the audio could not hold is 41, and the
  phase error measured a second later is 42. Values are in ms, offset by 10000.
- **Pads:**
  - A Beat Loop pad lights while the loop it started runs unchanged. The 4 BEAT button
    lights the 4-beat pad.
  - Beat Loop and Beat Jump pads light while held.
  - Held pads are tracked from the controller's own events: the 4 BEAT button's
    stand-in pad press has no release.

## Evidence

- **Host:** 7 new tests:
  - jumps back, forward, refused, and without a step either way, with the signal ramp;
  - the phase math;
  - pad LEDs;
  - SYNC with two decks: phase within 2 ms and no seek.

  The 8 legacy feature sets and target clippy for the 6 target sets pass.
- **Device** (`s73-beat-sync-*`, app `d7c86eb8…`), owner verdict "Radi":
  - jumps of −623, −1, −103 and −482 ms;
  - phase error a second later of −1, +1 and +1 ms;
  - underruns 0/0, `packet_errors=0`, longest fill 5.6 ms.

## Not covered

- SYNC is one-shot, as in the deck state. A later tempo change on the reference deck
  is not followed, and moving the synced deck's tempo fader turns SYNC off.
- Loops, hot cues and beat jumps still run without the grid, on the BPM alone.
