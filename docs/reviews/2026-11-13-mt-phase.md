# JC1060: Master Tempo stops that skip nothing

Date: 2026-11-13. Branch: `ogranak/jc1060-mt-phase`, from `main` at `2e685c3` (after
[continuous SYNC](2026-11-13-continuous-sync.md)).

## Problem

s84 showed SYNC phase errors of −29 and −30 ms a second after the soak DJ's SYNC, while
the phase checks were otherwise within ±1 ms.

A host test showed that Master Tempo's steady state is not the cause:
- SYNC against a deck in Master Tempo at +8 % lands at 0 ms;
- SYNC from such a deck lands at −1 ms.

The cause was the keylock stopping on the same source, as a scratch takes the deck or
Master Tempo goes off:
- It dropped the frames it had already taken from the reader but not played, up to
  about 34 ms, so the deck jumped forward by that much.
- A test reproduced it: after Master Tempo went off, 100 ms at +8 % moved the deck
  135 ms instead of 108.
- The soak DJ scratches often, so a jump landed inside a phase check's second now and
  then.

## Change

- When a deck's keylock stops on the same source, the reader first steps back through
  the history by the keylock's lag (`Reader::shift`, with its crossfade). The deck then
  goes on from what was heard, and nothing is skipped.
- This covers a scratch taking the deck (the step back happens before the scratch
  starts) and Master Tempo going off.
- On a new source (a load or a seek) the keylock still just starts over.

## Evidence

- **Host:** 2 new legacy engine tests:
  - Master Tempo going off at +8 % moves the deck 108 ms in 100 ms (it moved 135 ms
    before the fix);
  - SYNC against, and from, a deck in Master Tempo at +8 % lands within 3 ms.

  The legacy CI matrix, target clippy and the profile build pass.
- **Device:** s86 (app `d97934e1…`), owner verdict "Radi": no jump when Master Tempo goes
  off or a scratch starts, and SYNC against a Master Tempo deck stays together.
  - In 15 minutes: underruns 0/0, no panics, the USB task's free stack 2832 bytes.
  - One lost USB packet, the open lost-packet issue.
  - The phase checks ranged from −12 to +7 ms, against ±1 ms without Master Tempo. A
    grain's start moves up to ±256 frames (about 6 ms) to match the one before; that
    jitter shows in a single check. No check came near the earlier 30 ms.
  - Longest fill 7.0 ms, a little over the 6.7 ms accepted for Master Tempo with a
    scratch; the step back replays from the history, as a scratch does.

## Test plan

1. A deck in Master Tempo, tempo moved: Master Tempo off continues without a jump ahead.
2. A scratch while in Master Tempo: no jump or gap as it starts.
3. One deck in Master Tempo, SYNC on the other: the beats stay together.
