# The deck screen, page by page

Date: 2026-11-26. Branch: `ogranak/gui-pass`, from main `2f04d44`, after
[firmware updates from the browser (#174)](2026-11-25-web-remote-ota.md).

## How the pass was run

The owner went through the board's screen one page at a time, the screensaver included:

1. Each page was drawn on the PC as it was.
2. The owner chose what to change.
3. Each change went to the board as a play image through the Web Remote's firmware update.
4. The next page followed only once the owner had checked the current one on the board.

The images were s214 to s229.

**Tools:**

- **The palette** ([`docs/palette`](../palette/ruzina-screen-palette.png)):
  - every colour the screen uses, with the elements it is on, for the page before the pass
    and again after it;
  - for each colour, the value written and the value the RGB565 panel shows;
  - `make_palette.py` draws it.
- **The pages on the PC** (`rust-core/examples/render_screens.rs`):
  - it draws every page with the board's Slint renderer, fonts and images;
  - what Rust draws itself (waveforms, meters, the phase strip) is left out of it, and was
    sketched beside it for review.

  Run it with:

  ```sh
  cargo run --example render_screens --features "idf-soak-autoplay idf-slint-ui" -- OUT
  ```

## What changed

**Everywhere:**

- The background is black (it was `#100C08`): the screen, the lanes and overviews, the deck
  cards, the rail, the FX column and every page.
- Thin borders keep the parts apart.

**Screensaver and boot screen:**

- The screensaver is on black.
- RUZINA stands lit, and a soft band of the brightened word (`wordmark/word-shine.png`)
  slides across it every 4 s. The boot screen has the same shine.
- Under the word, one of three of the owner's sayings, a new one each minute.

**UPDATING FIRMWARE** is in bright red.

**Overview:**

- **Waveform:** the waveforms are in rekordbox's 3-band colours (blue, gold, white), with a
  red playhead.
- **Lanes and overviews:**
  - the lanes are 200 pixels high, at x 129, so their playhead falls on the middle of the
    gap between the deck cards;
  - on this page the deck cards are taller, with 80-pixel overviews;
  - the overviews show the Hot Cues as lines with flags.
- **Phase strip:** dark grey (`#202020`), with deck 1's beats in bright red, deck 2's in
  bright blue, and a white middle.
- **Buttons:**
  - PLAY and CUE are 50 pixels high: filled when on, an outline when not;
  - MT and Q are 44 by 34;
  - the covers are as wide as the buttons.
- **The time row:**
  - it is spaced out, with the time left and the BPM larger;
  - the time left blinks red in a track's last 30 s;
  - the key is in green above Q;
  - the deck's state (SYNC, LOOP, EMPTY) is a tag beside the title.

**Library:**

- **Rows:** on black, ten rows of 42 pixels with larger text, so a page is ten rows.
- **Headings:** on dark grey, with a rust line under them.
- **Right column:** from the top, LOAD D1 and D2 (the same size), BACK and the BPM filter,
  in the outline style.
- **Sorting:** new TRACK, ARTIST and BPM buttons sort as the headings do. A second tap turns
  the order over.
- **TIME column:** it is wider, so a time such as 10:24 is no longer cut.

**Hot Cues:** empty pads, MEMORY, DELETE and the memory list are on black.

**FX:**

- **Colour:** the effect's colour runs through the page: its button, the beat chosen and the
  depth.
- **Effect buttons:** each effect is outlined in its own colour.
- **Channels:** in the decks' colours.
- **FX ON:** a green outline when off, filled when on.

**Settings:** black backgrounds only; its buttons are as they were.

## A screen that stood still

**What happened:** on s214 the screen stood still when the screensaver came on, and on s215d
at boot. Audio, USB and the Web Remote went on.

**What s215d showed:** it logged the screen task's step and its PPA copies. The task was
waiting in a copy that never ended: 58 begun, 57 done. That copy was 134 by 112 pixels,
under the shine.

**The causes:**

- The screen waited for every PPA copy in the driver's blocking mode, without a limit.
- The shine asked for a small copy at every refresh.

**The fixes:**

- **Covers:** the boot screen, the screensaver and UPDATING FIRMWARE are drawn by the CPU.
  No deck plays under them.
- **Every PPA copy** (`display_touch.c`):
  - copies now run in the driver's non-blocking mode, waited for on our own semaphore for up
    to 50 ms;
  - a copy that does not end leaves the PPA to it, logs `ppa stuck=1`, and from then on the
    CPU draws, as it already did when a copy failed;
  - on s218d the shine's copies ran through the PPA this way for 4 minutes without one
    stuck.
