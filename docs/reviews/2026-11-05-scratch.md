# JC1060: scratch on the jog wheel

Date: 2026-11-05. Branch: `ogranak/jc1060-scratch`, from `main` at `bf671ce`.

## Problem

`ruzina-deck` already emitted `ScratchJog` while the jog top is touched, but the
legacy deck engine only counted it as unsupported. The shared
`ruzina-audio-scratch` crate has the platter model, but nothing in the legacy
path used it.

## Change

While the jog is touched, the deck's frames come from `ScratchState`, which
reads the played-audio history that loops already use.

- **Reader (`deck_play.rs`):**
  - A still platter gives silence, which does not count as an underrun.
  - Moving ahead of what was played pulls frames from the ring into the history.
  - On release, playback continues exactly from the head, through the history
    and then the ring, as after a loop.
  - A new source (load or seek) ends the gesture. If the jog is still touched,
    the next block starts a new one.
- **Engine (`deck_engine.rs`):** collects the ticks of the current gesture
  (checked against `scratch_epoch`) and hands them over once per render block,
  only for a loaded deck with no fault and no seek running.
- **Mix (`deck_mix.rs`):** during a gesture the deck renders even if paused, at
  one source frame per output frame with pitch 0. The platter sets the speed.
  EQ, filter and fader work as usual.
- **Shared crates:** unchanged. `ruzina-audio-scratch` is a new dependency of
  `deck-play`, built at opt-level 3.

### Limits

- **Reach:** back about 24 s from the last seek or load, forward about 1.5 s
  (the ring). At an edge the audio stops.
- **Paused deck:** stays paused after release, at the head's position.
- **Loop:** a wrap waits for the release, then works as usual.

## First device run (s33)

App `31607814…`, 852704 B, with the crate's default 250 frames per tick and no
fades.

- **Owner verdict:** too fast, with a click on both touch and release. Pause,
  loop and the other deck worked.
- **Log:**
  - Underruns 0/0, `packet_errors=0`, no faults.
  - Longest fill 4119 µs, and 94 of about 20000 fills over 3 ms. The longest
    fill rose during a fast scratch on deck 2 with both decks loaded. The fill
    budget is 6 ms.

## Fixes

- **Clicks:** the audio stepped when it started or stopped. Each change now goes
  through a 256-frame fade (5.8 ms), using the loop crossfade.
  - **Touch while playing:** the audio that would have followed fades out.
  - **Platter stops or starts, edge of the audio, release:** fades from the last
    frame played to whatever plays next.
  - **Touch while paused:** starts from silence, because the last frame played is
    old.
  - **Release while paused:** the frames stop as soon as the gesture ends, so the
    platter stops first and the gesture ends after its fade.
- **Speed:** frames per tick are now source rate × 1.8 s / 1024 ticks, about 77.5
  at 44.1 kHz. So one platter turn takes as long as a record at 33⅓ rpm.

## Not covered

- **Ticks per turn:** 1024 is an estimate. Two long moves in s33 had 1012 and
  1032 ticks, but the owner could not say whether each was one turn, and the
  exact measurement was not made. The speed is confirmed only by ear.
- **Release while a paused deck's platter moves, and the scratch in a loop:** the
  log cannot show a click or how the platter moved at release, so these rest on
  the owner's verdict. The gesture in the loop was short.
- **Deck 1 with both decks loaded:** every gesture in that part of s34 was on
  deck 2.
- **Why the fill dropped from 4119 to 3377 µs:** s33 ran about 3.2 times faster,
  which may explain it, but the two runs were not the same gestures.
- **Slow platter movement:** the crate holds the last speed for 3 windows
  (about 16 ms) after a tick, so very slow movement travels further than the
  ticks say. The owner did not report it, and the crate is unchanged.
- **App size:** the 24 KB growth was not explained.
