# JC1060: Rekordbox hot cues and a flash journal

Date: 2026-11-06. Branch: `ogranak/jc1060-hot-cues`, from `main` at `f47dace`.

## Change

Until now every track loaded with no cues, and `PersistHotCues` was only counted,
so a cue lived in RAM until the next load.

- **Rekordbox cues:** the startup pass already imported each track's analysis and
  dropped the result. It now keeps each track's bank in PSRAM for its loads.
- **Own cues:** they go to the journal format the native firmware uses, on a new
  1 MB `hotcues` partition at `0x110000`. On a load, a saved slot wins over a
  Rekordbox cue on the same pad.
- **When it writes:** only while no deck plays, because flash stalls all code
  running from it, USB audio included. Until then banks wait in RAM, one per track,
  16 tracks at most.
- **Owner:** the SD task does all journal reads and writes. A journal failure turns
  the journal off until the next boot and logs event 18; audio continues.
- **LEDs:** Hot Cue pads light when they hold a cue.

## Flashing

- `partitions-deck-engine.csv` keeps the earlier layout and adds the new partition.
- `s37-hot-cues-flash.py` erased the new partition once, after a backup, so old
  content cannot read as journal. Later flashes from the usual template do not touch
  it, so saved cues survive them.

## Evidence

- **Host:** 2 new tests. All 8 legacy feature sets and target clippy on 6 of them
  pass. The journal itself is covered by the `ruzina-hot-cues` tests, and the C
  flash calls run only on the board.
- **Build:** production `deck-engine`, app `db15d7dc…`, 870992 B of the 1 MB partition.
  Load chain about 51.7 KB, UAC fill chain 3344 B.
- **Device** (`s37-hot-cues-run-2`), owner verdict "Radi":
  - **In the log:** cues set and jumped to on both decks, then deleted with Shift +
    pad on both decks.
  - **Writes:** 12 banks reached flash with no failure, in 2 runs of 2 after a pause
    and then 8 deletes while paused. Longest 11.7 ms, shortest 3.1 ms.
  - **Audio:** underruns 0/0, `packet_errors=0`, no faults. Longest fill 3578 µs, 21 of
    29029 fills over 3 ms, none of 6 ms or more.

## Not covered

- **Cues after a reboot:** this log has no reboot and no track load, so reading a bank
  back from flash is not proven on the device.
- **Rekordbox cues on the pads:** nothing in the log shows which pads held them.
- **Sector erase:** no write took longer than 11.7 ms, so no write in this log seems to
  have erased a sector. Erasing takes longer and is still unmeasured, in silence by
  design.
- **Writing during a quick play:** the owner reported no gap, but the log does not show
  whether a write overlapped a press of Play.
- **Loss cases, by design:** power off while a deck plays loses cues waiting in RAM. In
  a long mix with no pause, a 17th track's new cues push out the oldest waiting bank.
