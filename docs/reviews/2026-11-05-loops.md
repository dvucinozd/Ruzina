# JC1060: seamless loops from a played-audio history

Date: 2026-11-05. Branch: `ogranak/jc1060-loops`, from `main` at `e7b1c57`.

## Problem

`ruzina-deck` already emitted `SetLoop` and `ClearLoop`, but the legacy deck
engine only counted them as unsupported, so no loop control did anything.

The decoder can loop (`StreamChange::Loop`), but that path does not suit the
legacy transport. The ring between the SD task and the mixer holds about 1.5 s
of audio ahead of the playhead. When a loop is set, audio past the loop end is
already in the ring. Going back through the decoder would restart the ring
epoch on every wrap, like a seek, with an audible gap.

## Change

All of this is in the legacy firmware. The shared crates are unchanged.

- **History (`deck_play.rs`):** each deck keeps its last 2^20 source frames
  (about 23.8 s at 44.1 kHz, 4 MiB) in PSRAM, in the same allocation as its
  ring.
  - `Reader::pop` stores every frame it takes from the ring, indexed by source
    frame.
  - Only the consumer touches this storage.
- **Loop playback (`Reader`):**
  - The first pass plays from the ring.
  - At the loop end the reader goes back to the loop start and replays the
    history, while the ring waits at the loop end.
  - On exit it plays the rest of the loop from the history and then continues
    from the ring, which already holds the audio after the loop.
  - A loop end moved past the ring position (Loop Double) plays on from the
    ring before wrapping.
  - The resampler, EQ and filter just receive frames from a different place.
- **Loop start not in the history:** this covers a start before the last seek
  or more than 2^20 frames back.
  - The reader plays through and reports it once.
  - The engine seeks to the loop start, with the usual cushion.
  - The loop stays set, so every later wrap replays the history.
- **Engine (`deck_engine.rs`, `deck_mix.rs`):**
  - `SetLoop` and `ClearLoop` reach the reader at the next render block.
  - A load, or losing the media, clears the loop.
  - The end-of-track stop waits while a loop runs.
  - The reported position follows the replay.
- **4 BEAT/EXIT:** the FLX4 button arrives as `ReloopExit`, which does nothing
  in `ruzina-deck` when there is no loop, and a shared test requires that.
  - The engine therefore translates a press with no active loop into the
    4-beat Beat Loop pad action.
  - With a loop running, the press exits as before.
  - **Trade-off:** this button no longer restores the last loop.
- **LEDs (`controller_midi.rs`):** Loop In lights from the press that marks the
  start. Loop Out lights once the loop runs. Both go off on exit.

**Memory:** free PSRAM after boot drops from about 28.5 MB to about 20.1 MB.

## Evidence

- **Host tests:** 7 new tests.
  - Numbered PCM frames prove the exact wrap, the exit into the ring, a Loop
    Out just behind the playhead, and Loop Double.
  - Two cover a loop start outside the history.
  - Two engine tests cover Loop In, Loop Out and exit, and 4 BEAT starting,
    exiting and starting again. Both check the LED state, no seek and no
    underrun.
  - Tests pass for `idf-media-bridge` 20, `idf-exfat-fixture` 29,
    `idf-media-load` 44, `idf-deck-play` 68, `idf-deck-mix` 69+24,

    `idf-soak-autoplay` 97+24. Clippy passes with `-D warnings` for all of them.

  - **Position:** on a track of about 90 BPM it jumped back exactly 2668 ms at

## Not covered on the device

- **Beat Loop pads:** no pad event appears in any of these logs.
- **Deck 2:** every loop press in the logs was on deck 1.
- **Loop start outside the history:** a loop longer than the history has not
  run on the board. One case was seen, in `s26`: after jog seeks while paused,
  Play reached the old loop's end and seeked once to its start.
- **Tempo and EQ during a loop:** not exercised on the device.
- **Click at the wrap:** the owner confirmed the section repeats. No log can
  show a click, and the host test proves only the frame sequence.

## Open

- **Reloop on the FLX4:** the button no longer restores the last loop, and no
  other button does yet.
- **Loop In and Out while a loop runs:** they follow `ruzina-deck` and were not
  examined on the device.
- **Scratch, pad FX and beat FX** are still counted as unsupported.
