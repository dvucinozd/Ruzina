# JC1060: Beat FX and Pad FX

Date: 2026-11-05. Branch: `ogranak/jc1060-fx`, on top of `ogranak/jc1060-scratch`.

## Change

`ruzina-audio-runtime` already had the effects, but the legacy mix built its
render graph without them and the engine counted `ApplyBeatFx` and `ApplyPadFx`
as unsupported.

- **Memory:** 1.5 MB of PSRAM, allocated with the rings by the SD owner. The USB
  task moves to the graph with effects outside the audio fill.
- **Controls:** the mix reads the FX from the deck state per block, and the Beat
  FX On LED follows it.
- **Shared code:**
  - `ruzina-audio-dsp` gains `WetFade`, a 5.3 ms wet/dry fade used by the Beat FX
    and pad filters. Switching them outright stepped by up to 3024 on a tone that
    steps by 261 per sample.
  - The flanger's wet level now falls to zero after it turns off.
  - These reach the native firmware too.

## Evidence

- **Host:** 3 new tests in `deck_engine/tests.rs`. All 8 legacy feature sets, target
  clippy on 6 of them, and the `ruzina-audio-dsp` and `ruzina-audio-runtime` tests
  and clippy pass.
- **Build:** production `deck-engine`, app `c4280a75…`, 856592 B. Load chain 50672 B,
  UAC fill chain 3168 B.
- **Device** (`s35-fx-*`), owner verdict "Radi":
  - **In the log:** Beat FX on once, with 2 effect changes and 1 beat change, plus
    depth moves. Pad FX bank 1, pads 1 to 4 on both decks. EQ and scratch on both
    decks during the run.
  - **Underruns 0/0, `packet_errors=0`, no faults.** Longest fill 3633 µs, 71 of 19028
    fills over 3 ms, none of 6 ms or more. The first fills were longer than without
    effects (2245 µs against 1296 µs in s34), and IRAM stays unneeded under the
    5 ms limit.

## Not covered

- **Not in the device log:** turning Beat FX off, the Delay effect, Pad FX bank 2,
  and the channel switch. Their behaviour is proven on the host only, except the
  channel switch, which has no test here.
- **Clicks:** the log cannot show them, so they rest on the owner's verdict. The
  host tests use a 1 kHz tone at one depth.
- **First repeat of Echo and Delay:** it arrives whole, with no fade, because the
  delay line starts empty.
- **Effect tails:** a seek or load resets the deck's effects, so the tail stops.
- **Pads 5 to 8:** the shared presets define only pads 1 to 4 in each bank.
- **Beat FX tempo:** with the channel switch on both, it follows deck 1. A BPM
  outside 40 to 300 counts as 120.
