# JC1060: the SD card is tried again when it fails to take its clock

Date: 2026-11-18. Branch: `ogranak/jc1060-sd-retry`, from `main` at `97d8da5`.

## Problem

On s85 and s115, the first boot after a flash could not bring up the SD card: it did not
take its clock (`failed to set clk`, `ESP_ERR_TIMEOUT`).
- `sd_bridge.c` tried `sdmmc_card_init` once, at 40 MHz, and on a failure gave up until
  the next restart.
- Since s116 a failure no longer takes the Wi-Fi's slot down with it
  ([SD slot](2026-11-16-sd-slot-teardown.md)), but the deck still had no music until a
  power-cycle.

The cause is not known. An outside review suggested switching the on-chip LDO 4 off and on
through `sd_pwr_ctrl_set_io_voltage`. That call only sets the IO voltage; it does not
power the card down, so it is not used here.

## Change

`main/sd_bridge.c`, in the SD owner's start-up:
- **Up to three tries** at bringing up the card (`SD_INIT_ATTEMPTS`).
- **Between tries:**
  - only the card's slot 0 is removed (`sdmmc_host_deinit_slot(0)`), and 100 ms later
    added back;
  - slot 1 belongs to the Wi-Fi's C6 and is never touched;
  - without the C6, the controller goes with its last slot, so `sdmmc_host_init` brings
    it back first. IDF reports `ESP_ERR_INVALID_STATE` when it is already up, and that is
    accepted.
- **The third try runs at 20 MHz** (`SDMMC_FREQ_DEFAULT`) instead of 40.
- **Logging:**
  - each failed try logs `rust_sd_card_init attempt=… max_khz=… error=…`;
  - `S6_RUST_SD_BRIDGE` now gives the attempts it took, and the maximum clock of the
    try that worked.

The older `sd_readonly.c` diagnostic is unchanged.

## Evidence

- **Build:** s132 (app `3293e844…`) builds from `main` without warnings.
- **Device:** s132 is flashed, its readback matches, and it booted cold.
  - `S6_RUST_SD_BRIDGE … max_khz=40000 real_khz=40000 … attempts=1`, with no failed
    try.
  - The catalog read its 25 tracks.
  - `packet_errors=0`, underruns 0/0.
  - Owner: "Radi".
- **Not shown:** the retry path itself. The failure came on two boots in over a hundred,
  and cannot be caused on purpose. The first boot that fails will log its tries.

## Test plan

1. Boot normally: `attempts=1` at 40 MHz, and the catalog loads.
2. When a boot after a flash logs `rust_sd_card_init attempt=1 … failed to set clk`, a
   later try should bring the card up, and the deck should play without a power-cycle.
