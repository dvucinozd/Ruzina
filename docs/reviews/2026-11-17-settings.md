# JC1060: settings the deck keeps

Date: 2026-11-17. Branch: `ogranak/jc1060-settings-saved`, from `main` at `b7b5af8`.

## Problem

- **Nothing to set.** The Settings page only showed what the deck reports.
- **Fixed behaviour:**
  - LOAD LOCK was always on.
  - Both jogs always scratched (VINYL).
  - The screen always started at 25 %.
  - MAIN had no trim.
- **The shared crate was unused on the board.** `ruzina-settings` (RP-11) already had the
  record format and a power-cut-safe flash store, but the board had no partition for it
  and no owner.

## Change

### The settings

The Settings page gets a SETTINGS part under the decks' figures:

| Setting | Choices | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| LOAD LOCK | ON / OFF | ON refuses a load onto a playing deck, as before. |
| JOG DECK 1, JOG DECK 2 | VINYL / CDJ | CDJ: touching the platter's top neither holds nor scratches the deck (the engine's `JogCdj`). |
| BRIGHTNESS | - / + by 5 % | From 10 to 50 %, at once. |
| MAIN TRIM | 0 / -3 / -6 dB | MAIN's gain before the limiter. |

- **Brightness cap:** the board still runs from the PC's USB port. A saved brightness its
  supply cannot hold would come back at every boot, so the page stops at 50 %.
- **Out of the box:**
  - the shared defaults, with LOAD LOCK on and VINYL on both decks;
  - the screen at 25 %, as it has always started;
  - the deck's Wi-Fi on.

  Settings the board does not offer (time display, cue mode, DJ Link) keep what the record
  holds.
- **Deck figures:** each deck's figures now fit on one line (source rate, buffered audio,
  underruns), to make room.

### How a change travels

1. **The page:** a tap changes the value in force (`deck_settings`, one packed atomic).
   The brightness follows at once (`ruzina_display_backlight`).
2. **The USB client task:** on its next turn, `Mix::apply_settings` hands LOAD LOCK and the
   jogs to the engine, and the trim to MAIN.
3. **The app main task:** once a second, after its heartbeat, it saves once the change has
   been quiet for 2 seconds and no deck plays, as Hot Cue saves wait.
   - Its stack is internal RAM, as flash operations need.
   - It runs with or without a card.
   - A failed save is logged (event 33), and saving stops until the next boot.

### Flash

- **Partition:** a 16 KiB `settings` partition (subtype 0x41) behind the Hot Cue journal,
  in both partition tables: `0xD10000` with the Slint UI, `0x210000` without it.
- **Boot:** before the screen and USB audio start, the board reads the record and publishes
  it. It then erases the sector the saves will move on to (logged as `settings state=…`).
  The screen starts at the saved brightness.
- **The store appends:** `ruzina-settings` records no longer replace each other in two
  slots. A write appends its record after the newest one in the same sector:
  - header and payload first, then the commit word;
  - 64 settings records fit in a sector;
  - only a full or damaged sector moves on to the other one, which is erased then unless
    `erase_spare` already did;
  - records the two-slot store wrote still load, and the next record goes after them.
- **No erase while USB audio flows:** an erase stalls the fill and the screen whether or not
  a deck plays.
  - A save that would erase (once 64 saves since boot fill a sector) waits until USB audio
    has stood still for a second, that is, no controller yet, or it is unplugged. It is
    logged once as event 34; the setting stays in force meanwhile.
  - After a save, the next spare sector is erased ahead in the same window (event 35).
  - The store tells beforehand whether a write would erase (`write_erases`).

## Evidence

- **Host:**
  - **`ruzina-settings` store tests:**
    - records append without an erase;
    - a full sector moves on, and the spare is erased ahead;
    - a power cut at every byte keeps the previous record, with and without a sector
      change;
    - two-slot records still load and append;
    - a damaged record is stepped over, and a header with no length ends the sector;
    - all 18 crate tests pass, as do the owner's power-cut tests.
  - **`deck_settings` tests:**
    - packing;
    - the quiet wait and the clock's wrap;
    - the brightness range;
    - the trim's decibels.
  - **A new engine test:**
    - LOAD LOCK off admits a load onto the playing deck;
    - CDJ leaves the playing deck running under a touched platter, and VINYL holds it again;
    - −6 dB halves MAIN.
  - **Other checks:**
    - the `idf-deck-engine`, `idf-soak-autoplay idf-slint-ui` and
      `idf-soak-autoplay idf-web-remote` tests and clippy;
    - target clippy for every CI feature set;
    - the media catalog and the desktop simulator's tests.
- **Device:**
  - **s128 (app `c5f762ef…`), with the earlier two-slot store:**
    - The first boot read no record (`state=0`) and erased the spare in 34 ms.
    - The page's settings worked. Owner: "Radi".
    - The second and third saves each erased a sector (50 and 55 ms). One fill took 27 ms,
      2 USB packets were lost, and the owner saw the screen flash once.
  - **s129 (app `39303711…`), with the appending store:**
    - The boot read s128's record (`state=1`) and erased the other sector in 27 ms.
    - Five saves, no erase, `packet_errors=0`, longest fill 5.2 ms, underruns 0/0.
    - The screen stayed steady. Owner: "Radi".
  - **Review follow-up, s130 (app `2a75085d…`):**
    - Saves moved from the card's load task to the app main task, so they also happen
      without a card.
    - A save that would erase, and the spare's erase, wait for USB audio to stand still.
    - On the board: the app main task saved twice (9.4 and 2.2 ms, 5.7 KiB of its stack
      left), and the next cold boot read the brightness back as 30 %.
    - Four cold boots, `packet_errors=0`. Owner: "Radi".

## Test plan

1. Change each setting on the page: LOAD LOCK, both jogs, the brightness, the trim.
2. With the decks stopped, wait a few seconds, then cold-boot the board: the settings come
   back.
3. CDJ on a deck: touching the platter does not stop it. VINYL: it does.
4. LOAD LOCK off: a track loads onto a playing deck.
5. Change settings a few times: the screen does not flash, and the log shows no
   `packet_errors`.
