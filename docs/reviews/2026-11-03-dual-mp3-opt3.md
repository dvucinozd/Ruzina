# JC1060: two MP3 decks play clean with the decode path at opt-level 3

Date: 2026-11-03. Branch: `ogranak/jc1060-source-supply-profile` (PR #82).
Native minimum 302 and APTA unchanged.

## Change

- **Cause:** at the legacy release default `opt-level = "s"`, one MP3 deck took
  about 47 % of core 1 in decoder steps (466710 µs per source second, from the
  [continuous campaign](2026-11-03-continuous-device.md)). Two decks plus SD
  reads did not fit, and the rings drained.
- **Fix:** `rust-core/Cargo.toml` now builds `nanomp3-core`, `nanoaudio-mp3` and
  `ruzina-audio-runtime` at `opt-level = 3`. Every other crate stays at `"s"`.
  - The host benchmark gives about 10× faster decoder steps with bit-exact PCM
    ([source budget](2026-11-03-source-budget.md)).
  - The [in-place MP3 reset](2026-11-03-mp3-in-place-reset.md) keeps the
    decoder construction out of large stack frames.
- **CI fix:** the `legacy_codec_streams` test uses `ruzina_mixer`, so it now
  requires `deck-mix`. Before this it broke the `idf-deck-play` job in
  `media-bridge-host`.

## Follow-up: USB audio resilience (2026-11-04)

**Failure.** After the first run, the board was silent although MIDI and the
display still worked.
- Each lone isochronous packet error had triggered a full endpoint
  halt/flush/clear and restart.
- The fourth restart on one connection exceeded `UAC_RECOVERY_LIMIT` and left
  the stream in `FAULT_HELD` until the controller was reconnected.
- The log shows the three earlier restarts: `recovery_prefill count=12`.

**Fix.**
- **`uac_driver.inc`:** a lost isochronous packet is only counted
  (`packet_errors`), and the stream carries on. Transfer-level failures still
  recover.
- **`uac_recovery.h`:** after 1000 clean transfers (about 6 s) the recovery
  budget returns to zero.
- **Optimisation:** `ruzina-audio-dsp` and `ruzina-mixer` also build at
  opt-level 3.
- **Tests:** the C driver and recovery tests pass (8). Two new cases check
  that a lost packet does not restart the stream and that a clean stream
  earns back its budget.

**Build.**
- The app is 814640 B (77.7 %). SHA-256 is
  `ec46fa9531e6ce04bc353db2576ad537d29260ded67610163428b6afc47d05b1`.
- The load chain is 50704 B and the UAC fill chain 3056 B.

**Device run** (`s11-uac-resilience-*` in `D:\BoardEvidence`).
- **Owner test and verdict ("bilo je sve ok"):** both MP3 decks together;
  hot cue and CUE seeks while playing; a track swap on deck 2.
- **Streaming:** 51000 transfers, about 5 minutes, with one stream start.
  The single `packet_errors=1` caused no restart, and there was no
  `FAULT_HELD`.
- **Underruns:** 11 on deck 1, all in the window right after four hot cue
  seeks and one CUE. This is the known cold-MP3-seek cost, and deck 2 had 0.
  - **Fill time:**

    | Measure | First run | This run |
    |---|---:|---:|
    | `fill_max_us` | 5325 µs | 4848 µs |
    | Callbacks above 3 ms | 5.9 % | 3.4 % |

## Open

- **Seek underruns:** MP3 seeks underrun briefly, 11 blocks after a burst of
  seeks. The fix is the MP3 seek index walk.
- **Fill time:** `fill_max_us` is still 4848 µs, above the 3000 µs headroom
  target. Next comes the `uac-profile` substage timing.
  - The deck filter already recomputes its coefficients only when the control
    moves, so it is not the obvious cost.
  - The fill path still runs `ruzina-audio-dsp` and `ruzina-mixer` at `"s"`.
    The next candidates are the existing `uac-profile` substage timing, or
    adding those two crates to opt-level 3 and measuring again.
- **Not tested on the board:**
  - WAV and FLAC on both decks at once;
  - a soak longer than 5 minutes.
