# JC1060: Beat FX by touch

Date: 2026-11-12. Branch: `ogranak/jc1060-fx-touch`, from `main` at `6f6c43b` (after the
[Hot Cues page](2026-11-12-hot-cues-page.md)).

## Problem

The FX panel right of the lanes showed the Beat FX (effect, channel, beat, depth, on),
but only the controller could change them. Picking an effect or a beat meant stepping
through them one button press at a time.

## Change

- **FX page:** a tap on the FX panel opens a BEAT FX page over the lanes and meters, as
  the Library and Hot Cues pages do. No tab is marked while it is open. It has:
  - EFFECT: Filter, Echo, Flanger, Delay;
  - BEAT: 1/4, 1/2, 1, 2, 4;
  - CHANNEL: deck 1, deck 2, both;
  - DEPTH: a bar to tap or drag along, with the percentage;
  - a large FX ON / FX OFF button;
  - CLOSE, back to the Overview.

  What is in use is lit, from the engine's Beat FX, so the controller's changes show
  too.
- **Same path as the controller:** every tap becomes the controller's Beat FX events:
  - The controller only steps the effect and the beat, so the USB task takes the
    engine's Beat FX at that moment and sends as many Select Next, or Beat Inc or Dec,
    presses as reach the pick.
  - CHANNEL sends the channel switch's value, DEPTH the knob's absolute value, and
    FX ON a press.
  - A drag sends only depths the engine does not already have, so it does not flood
    the touch queue.
- **Touch events:** `touch_events` takes the Beat FX state and returns up to 8 events.
  A small builder replaces the fixed pair.
- **Hot Cues page:** s80 is the first image with the DELETE fix from the
  [Hot Cues page](2026-11-12-hot-cues-page.md) Codex review, already on `main`.

## Evidence

- **Host:** 1 new test, and 1 extended:
  - new: from the FX page, every effect, beat and channel is reached from wherever the
    engine is, the depth is set exactly, and FX ON toggles;
  - extended: the touch queue round-trips each FX touch.

  The legacy feature sets and target clippy (including `idf-slint-ui` and the profile
  build) pass.
- **Device:** s80 (app `31a4b3d2…`), owner verdict "Radi" on all six test points,
  including the DELETE fix.
  - The 15-minute capture: underruns 0/0, no panics, one lost USB packet
    (`packet_errors=1`, the open lost-packet issue).
  - Longest fill 22.2 ms, once, at 591 s. It fell in the same log interval as a 45 ms
    Hot Cue journal save, made during the soak DJ's Pause All. The fill most likely
    waited on that flash write; a later 52 ms save caused no such fill. The ring's
    cushion covered it, so nothing was heard.
    - Every other fill stayed at or under 6.2 ms.
    - s77 to s79 saved Hot Cues 13 to 18 times each, up to 37 ms, without such a fill.
    - This change does not touch the save or the fill. A fill that waits on a long
      journal write is a separate open item.

## Test plan

1. A tap on the FX panel opens BEAT FX; the lanes and meters are hidden.
2. EFFECT, BEAT and CHANNEL change the effect and light the pick; with FX ON it is heard.
3. Dragging along DEPTH changes the depth, and the percentage follows the finger.
4. FX ON and OFF from the screen and from the controller follow each other.
5. CLOSE brings back the Overview, with lanes and meters.
6. On HOT CUES, DELETE goes out even when the pad is held longer.
